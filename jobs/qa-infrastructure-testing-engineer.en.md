# QA / Infrastructure Testing Engineer

## Gonka External Test Lab & Community DevNet

**Remote · Contract**

We are looking for **a QA / Infrastructure Testing Engineer** to join Gonka's External Test Lab and Community DevNet.

You will work hands-on with distributed nodes and services in production-like environments, helping validate network upgrades, inference and Proof-of-Compute flows, host and broker interactions, and other critical changes before they reach mainnet.

This is not a conventional manual QA role. We are looking for an engineer who can deploy infrastructure, investigate system behavior through logs and chain queries, design practical validation strategies, and clearly determine whether a release is ready for production.

### About the project

[Gonka](https://gonka.ai) is a decentralized AI compute network that connects developers with independent GPU hosts, providing OpenAI-compatible access to AI inference without relying on a single centralized cloud provider. 

The community has funded an independent testing initiative to validate protocol changes on a dedicated, geographically distributed DevNet before they reach mainnet. Test plans, reports, and defect write-ups are public, so your work will be visible to and used by the entire ecosystem.

### Responsibilities

- Contribute to network quality standards and the overall test strategy.
- Define acceptance criteria and test plans for critical network components and lifecycle events.
- Build pre-release validation frameworks, including smoke tests and regression coverage for known failure patterns.
- Deploy and verify distributed nodes and service stacks in production-like environments.
- Validate critical end-to-end flows, including:
  - participant registration and key management;
  - delegated permissions and fee grants;
  - inference and Proof-of-Compute participation;
  - host, broker, gateway, and proxy behavior.
- Use health signals, chain queries, metrics, and logs as primary validation inputs.
- Verify upgrade readiness, rollback feasibility, and post-change health across the stack.
- Establish, tune, and document practical operational settings based on DevNet experience, including PoC and inference validation thresholds.
- Contribute relevant findings and operational guidance directly to the official Gonka documentation where appropriate.
- Support DevNet validation and release-readiness assessments before production rollouts.
- Validate recovery procedures and incident resolution through root-cause analysis and re-testing.
- Report defects with clear reproduction steps, impact assessments, supporting evidence, and release-blocking status.
- Track and communicate quality metrics reflecting network health and operational reliability.

### Required experience

- 3+ years of experience in system QA, Test Engineering, SDET, or quality-focused DevOps/SRE.
- Understanding of blockchain lifecycle processes, key management, and authentication flows, including registration, delegated permissions, and fee grants.
- Experience operating and validating blockchain nodes, preferably Cosmos SDK-based, including synchronization, recovery, RPC queries, and network behavior across different phases.
- Experience validating distributed systems in production-like environments.
- Strong test design skills, including test plans, acceptance criteria, smoke, regression, end-to-end, edge-case, and negative testing.
- Solid Linux, SSH, shell scripting, and log-based verification skills.
- Experience with Docker and container orchestration, including environment and configuration reload behavior.
- Technical documentation skills, including defect reports, runbooks, test results, and sign-off checklists.
- Comfort with time-boxed validation before critical network events.

### Nice to have

- SDET experience with scripted validation, CI pipelines, or automated health checks.
- Blockchain/Web3 QA experience, including testnet operations, bridge testing, wallet and key flows, and upgrade regression.
- Cross-chain or EVM testnet experience, including withdrawal flows and contract interactions.
- Experience with GPU/ML inference testing, including worker health, model serving, and artifact delivery.
- Experience with coordinated multi-node upgrades.
- API gateway and proxy testing, including failures, latency, and request tracing.
- Experience with decentralized inference or Proof-of-Compute networks.
