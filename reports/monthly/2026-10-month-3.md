## Testing and findings

### Host lifecycle and recovery

Independent JOIN, backup, reset and restore runs identified reproducible defects. Fixes addressed consensus-key registration and recovery, interrupted identity handling, portable operator execution, preflight/state-sync behavior and retained diagnostics. The September incident is closed in [#143](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/143); the consensus-key defect is documented in [#141](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/141). Broader lifecycle and independent-upgrade validation remains tracked on the task board.

### Escrow distribution and settlement

- **Participant distribution:** the study reproduced all **221 real escrows** in a mainnet epoch slot for slot, then simulated 1,000 escrows per model at group sizes 16, 32 and 64. The results distinguish inclusion probability from slot share and retain the model-specific weights ([#186](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/186), [tooling](../../testing/gonka-check/README.md)).
- **Same-epoch parameter change:** on October 4, an escrow created with group size 5 settled using its own five slots after governance changed the global size to 9. A new escrow used nine slots; both settled in the same epoch, payouts plus refunds matched deposits, and the parameter was restored to 5. This was one run through gateway A on DevShard v5 with `Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B`, not a v6 settlement result ([#187](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/187)).
- **PoC admission regression:** changing epoch length exposed a gateway fence calculated from block-height modulo rather than the actual epoch start. The defect blocked requests during inference and missed part of the intended PoC fence; the fix and regression coverage are in [#197](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/197) / [PR #205](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/pull/205).

### DevShard v6: regression, Gherkin and load

The upstream manual plan is written in **Gherkin**, with 24 scenarios and 42 expanded cases. The Lab mapped those scenarios to an isolated local test environment and recorded PASS, FAIL, partial and not-run outcomes ([#231](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/231)). That environment uses real DevShard processes with mock chain and model components; its results do not establish live-network acceptance.

Local catch-up and miss-rate testing found reproducible failures. Revalidation of successive upstream candidates showed improvements but retained ghost/orphaned-work failures; related upstream investigations and fixes include [gonka#1993](https://github.com/gonka-ai/gonka/pull/1993) and [gonka#2003](https://github.com/gonka-ai/gonka/pull/2003). The separate logprobs-switch regression passed its scoped checks; host-to-gateway payload behavior was verified indirectly, as stated in the results ([#246](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/246)).

On October 15, two sequential five-minute SSE runs on live DevNet outside regular and confirmation PoC completed without errors, using `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507` ([run results](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/242#issuecomment-6202732845)):

- **Gateway B:** 1,202 / 1,202 successful requests over 302.19 seconds — **3.98 requests/s**.
- **Gateway A:** 1,125 / 1,125 successful requests over 307.54 seconds — **3.66 requests/s**.

JSON and SSE smoke passed before both runs. These are measured closed-loop throughput points, not a fixed-rate 4 RPS guarantee. Earlier failed or blocked windows remain in the record. Phase, capacity, escrow and nonce checks are required before subsequent runs.
Full live Gherkin qualification, shared-storage/two-replica HA and agreed host-pause/fault exercises remain open ([#224](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/224), [#225](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/225), [#242](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/242), [#254](https://github.com/paranjko/external-test-lab/issues/254)).
